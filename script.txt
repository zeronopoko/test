*I walk down the slope and join the family by the water, my sleeves swinging with each step. The cup of fruit juice Isshin pressed into my hand at the vendor’s stall is empty. My cheeks feel warm, and my feet seem lighter than they did an hour ago.*

Ichigo pushes the banana away. “I’m good, Yuzu. Seriously, I don’t want any.”

Yuzu’s face crumples. She plants her feet in the grass, her voice shrill enough to carry across the riverbank. “Why don’t you want it?!”

Heads turn. Ichigo throws both hands up, half pleading and half trying to shield himself. “Yuzu, please! Lower your voice!” He glances at her flushed cheeks, then at Karin, who sways a little as she licks chocolate off her thumb. “...Wait. Are you guys drunk?”

By now, Ichigo’s friends have made their way down the slope. Keigo and Mizuiro stand nearby, with Sado beside them. Orihime clasps her hands to her chest, while Tatsuki, her right arm in a sling, hangs a step behind the others. None of them says a word.

“I,” Isshin announces, sweeping an arm wide and nearly losing his balance, “am extremely drunk!”

“What?!” Ichigo rounds on him. “Why are you proud of that?!”

“Because it isn’t our fault!” Isshin presses a hand to his chest, swaying. “Old Man Ken put out a fruit juice stall and mixed sake into the drinks instead of water! We drank three cups before anyone noticed!” He wags a finger. “Must be senility. Poor old Ken. His mind’s going.”

Keigo finally finds his voice. “I don’t think senility had anything to do with it.”

“He knew exactly what he was drinking, didn’t he?” Mizuiro says in a low voice, exchanging a knowing look with Keigo.

Ichigo drops back onto the grass and drags both hands down his face. “Great. That’s just great.”

Tatsuki and Orihime come down the slope a few steps behind the others. Tatsuki’s eyes pass over the tangle of limbs before settling on me, standing beside Ichigo and his sisters. I reach over and poke Ichigo in the arm.

“Hey.” Tatsuki tilts her chin at me. “Who’s the kid?”

Everyone looks.

“Oh!” Isshin flings an arm around my shoulders so suddenly that I stagger, then beams at the group. “Allow me to introduce the newest member of the Kurosaki family! This is Mochi-chan! Ichigo’s daughter!”

The riverbank falls silent.

Keigo’s mouth drops open. Mizuiro’s eyebrows climb. Orihime’s hands fly to her cheeks, and even Sado’s eyes widen a fraction. Keigo finds his voice first.

“Ichigo... went missing for ten days... to come back with a *daughter?!*”

“She is NOT my daughter!” Ichigo springs to his feet, scattering grass from his shirt. “I had nothing to do with it! Dad’s drunk, don’t listen to him!”

*I slip out from under Isshin’s arm and take a bouncy step forward. My cheeks are warm, and I feel a little light on my feet. For once, the words come easily.*

“Hello, everyone! I’m Mochi. Nice to meet you!”

For a moment, they simply stare. Then Orihime’s eyes light up, and she bows enthusiastically. “Nice to meet you too, Mochi-chan! I’m Orihime!”

Tatsuki raises an eyebrow, a small smile tugging at the corner of her mouth. Keigo presses both hands to his chest, still looking utterly baffled, while Mizuiro tilts his head with an amused smile. Sado gives a slow nod.

Ichigo stands with one arm half-raised, staring at me as though he’s trying to figure out what just happened. His gaze shifts between me and the others before his shoulders finally slump.

“...Huh. This is way better than the staring.” He rubs his forehead. “Why couldn’t you be like this all week?”

“I have tickets!” Isshin booms, clapping his hands together before fishing a handful of slips from inside his kimono. He waves them overhead. “I reserved the best seats in the house at seven o’clock this morning! Everyone, gather round!”

*I move in with the others, staying beside Ichigo's sisters as everyone crowds around Isshin to see the tickets.*

Ichigo looks at his father, then pauses.

Isshin puffs out his chest, clearly pleased with himself. “I really wanted to watch the fireworks from a raft on the river, but we almost got arrested by the cops!” He bursts into laughter.

Ichigo stares at him for a long moment.

“They should’ve thrown you in jail.”

Isshin’s attention shifts to Orihime and Tatsuki, who are hanging back at the edge of the group. He spreads his arms in an exaggerated bow.

“And how about you lovely ladies?”

“We’ll be there later,” they answer together.

“Okay! Great!”

Isshin charges off, with Karin, Yuzu, Keigo, Mizuiro, and Sado trailing after him. *I scamper along with them, my borrowed sandals slapping against the dirt and my kimono sleeves flapping with every step.*

Ichigo hangs back by the water. He scratches the back of his neck and turns to Tatsuki.

“Sorry about the chaos. Seriously. It’s fine if you don’t want to come.”

“I’ll show up later.” Tatsuki shrugs her good shoulder. “Go on. Catch up with them.”

Ichigo hesitates for a moment, still looking sheepish, then nods and jogs along the riverbank after the rest of us.
